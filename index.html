About us

We're an IT consultancy.
Try not to hold that against us.

We're about 40 people. Not 4,000 — so you won't get handed off to a junior in another timezone after the kickoff meeting. The people you talk to are the people who build your thing.

We work across the full stack: software development, front and back-end, web, and AI. We've been around long enough to know that most bad projects don't fail because of bad code. They fail because nobody said the uncomfortable thing in week one.

We say the uncomfortable thing in week one.

If you're looking for a vendor who nods along and disappears until the deadline — we're genuinely not the right fit. But if you want a team that treats your problem like it's worth thinking about, we might get along very well.

How we work

No discovery theater.
Just straight to the point.

Four steps. No fluff. The uncomfortable question happens in step one, not step four.

Step 01
The straight conversation

No deck, no 90-minute discovery call theater. Just a direct conversation about what you're trying to solve — and what might actually be the real problem.

Step 02
We push back (a bit)

We challenge the brief. Ask the annoying questions. Suggest what shouldn't be built yet. This is the part most vendors skip — it's the part we think matters most.

Step 03
We build it, properly

The same senior people you met start building. Regular updates that are actually useful. No surprises, no scope creep disguised as "enhancements."

Step 04
We ship. You keep it.

We hand over something that works, that your team understands, and that doesn't require us to stick around forever just to keep the lights on.
